How to use this pack 💛

  • Print on plain A4 — no color printer needed.
  • Slip a page into a sleeve + use a dry-erase marker to reuse forever.
  • Sit together for 5–10 mins. Praise the effort, not the result.
  • No pressure! If they wander off, that's totally fine. 🙂
